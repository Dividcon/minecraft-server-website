01 / BUSHWHACKSURVIVAL

WHAT IS BUSHWHACK?

BushwhackSurvival is a community-driven Minecraft survival SMP built around freedom, exploration, and creativity.

Start with nothing and build your way up. Establish a home, create settlements, trade with other players, explore the wilderness, and leave your mark on a world shaped by its community.

Inspired by medieval worlds and the machinery of the industrial age, Bushwhack combines classic Minecraft survival with a distinct medieval-steampunk atmosphere.
