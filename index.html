05 / QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

What is OceanSurvival? +

OceanSurvival is an ocean-only Minecraft survival server. The idea is simple: there is no normal mainland to rely on, so players have to build, explore and survive around the ocean.

What can I build? +

Anything you can build in Minecraft. Islands, underwater bases, ports, ships, towns, pirate fleets and huge ocean projects are all part of the idea.

Is it pay-to-win? +

No. OceanSurvival is designed around survival gameplay rather than selling advantages that decide who wins.

How do I join? +

Connect to play.oceansurvival.net on Java Edition. You can also join the Discord for announcements and community information.

Is Bedrock supported? +

Bedrock players can join OceanSurvival as well. Check the Discord for the latest connection details and compatibility information.
